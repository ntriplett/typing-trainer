# Game ideas (brainstorm, 2026-10-24)

What makes a game a game here: the word you type *is* the choice (type a
crow's word to shoo that crow, type a move's word to use it). Every key
is still practice, but it also commits you to something.

## Ground rules (agreed)

- Time pressure is fine in some games, not all, and never very hard.
- Soft losing: you can lose, but you keep trying; score = number of successes.
- About 1 minute per level, about 5 levels, 5-10 minutes of game time per session.
  Longer play might be earned later.
- Self-contained games first. Persistent / roguelite-with-unlocks later.
- Favorites to borrow loops from: Pokemon, Mario, Minecraft, Portal,
  Stardew Valley, shapez 2, Banished, Mario Kart.

## Built

- **Crow Patrol** (real-time). Crows from every side carry words; type to shoo. Six crow kinds
  (little, crow, big, caller, squirrel, golden), one new per round; slips make a crow call a friend.
  Arcade tally per round (pumpkins worth 10 crows); upgrades between rounds.
- **Pet Battles** (turn-based). Pick a partner (Leaf/Splash/Ember types) for each critter; each type
  has its own moves, each critter its own trick; a bag of items; tally and coins per win.
- **Farm Stand** (persistent, 2026-10-28). Plant by typing seed words, then 4 market hours of
  animal customers (rolled like shop pets) ordering veggies. The farm is saved: stand money,
  reputation, upgrades and decorations, paced to keep growing past 50 plays. A 3-star day gives
  a veggie for your pets. See PLAN.md 11m.

## Later
- **Train Switchyard** (real-time puzzle). Trains roll toward junctions; type a station
  word to route the next train. Wrong station costs stars.
- **Snowball Fort** (real-time). Short word = quick throw, long word = big throw that
  breaks their fort; type "duck" to dodge.
- **Trail Map Adventure** (roguelite). Map of forks, each path shows its word and
  risk/reward; type to choose. Events are short typing challenges; limited stamina.
  Reuses the hike art.
- **Potion Shop** (puzzle). Customers ask for effects; type ingredient names to brew.
  Ingredients add or cancel traits; buy rare ones with coins.
- **Fishing Derby**. Choose shallows (easy words, small fish) or deep (hard words, big
  fish, line snaps after 3 misses). Sell fish for a better rod. Builds on the fishing scene.
- **Cozy Village Builder** (persistent). Typing gathers wood/stone/food; choose what to
  build (house -> villagers, mill -> bread). Banished/Stardew flavor.
- **Word Tower** (wordplay, later). A tray of taught letters; build words to stack a
  tower, leftover letters pile up as junk. Needs spelling, so later.
- Ideas from the favorites list: a Mario Kart-style race with item boxes earned by
  typing streaks; a shapez-style factory where typed words route parts.
- **Tower Defense** type words to build new obsticals to defend your home
