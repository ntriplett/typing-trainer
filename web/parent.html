For grown-ups

Each player practices a little every day. Reaching the daily goal pays 2 arcade tokens, and half as much again pays 1 more; then practice rests for 12 hours. The lesson brain picks every lesson: warmups, new letters, and practice aimed at weak spots.

Keyboard help

      

        The keyboard with hands is free, and fades as each key is learned: first letters and lights at once,
        then later lights, blank keys, only the finger, only the hands, then no keyboard. It comes back
        for a while when a lesson goes badly.

Keys in practice

      

        Went ahead too fast? Click a learned key to hold it back: it and its lesson partner leave practice and
        the games, and once every other key is solid they're taught again with the full keyboard help. What
        they opened stays open. Click a held-back key to put it straight back.
