Wanderpaw lab

The whole world with no fog and no walls in your way. Look at any seed, click a place to open its small map, click a small hex to move yourself there (in your own world), or compare every land's feel in the gallery.

Place

Click a place on the map.

Pets

Your pets that can adventure. Give XP to test levels and second types (picked in the game's party list). Gates, temples and nooks add up the team's type levels.

    Gate levels

    The type levels a party needs for the gates between Camp and each place (each gate wants its number in levels of its type). When different ways need different types, the map shows the cheapest; click a place for all of them.

    Land gallery

    One small map of every land, from this seed's world (or the next few). Click one to open it.
